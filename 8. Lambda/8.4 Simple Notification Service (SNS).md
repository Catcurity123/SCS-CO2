#### A. What is it
(+) Public AWS Service - network connectivity with Public Endpoint
(+) Coordinates the sending and delivery of messages, messages are <= `256KB` payloads
(+) `SNS Topics` are the base entity of SNS - `permissions` and `configuration`
(+) A `publisher` sends messages to a `Topic`
(+) `Topics` have `Subscribers` which `receive`

###### What is the basis of SNS
(+) Delivery Status, including HTTP, Lambda, SQS
(+) Delivery Retries - Reliable Deliver
(+) It is regionally HA and scalable
(+) Support Server Side Encryption (SSE)
(+) Cross-Account via `Topic Policy (SNS Topic resource policy)`
